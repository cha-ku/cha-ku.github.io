# Weekly Notes 15/2026

Sorry for missing this last week - I was struck with The Plague (Common Cold).

I got the flu right at the start of the Easter Holiday long weekend so incredible timing there. I still feel slightly under the weather, but getting better now.

Anyway, last week I went to an [Algorave](https://luma.com/d1kq9csx)! I was a guitar-music-is-the-real-deal person but lately I've enjoyed quite a bit of Ambient electronic music, [Aphex Twin](https://youtu.be/CJsiDDI-7Xw?si=cWLBGHydyCNvU7O5) so I wanted to give it a try. Oh boy I'm glad I did.
I went to this thing with my friend Tom and a couple of Tom's colleagues. I had a great time getting introduced to this form of creative expression. There were a few sets for first timers for half an hour 
or so and after that, there were a couple of sets from the [pastagang](https://www.pastagang.cc/) collective. A 'set' consisted of 2 creatives performing together - one on the video side, generating
procedural visuals, usually with [Hydra](https://hydra.ojack.xyz/) and other generating the audio with [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/), although tools are not a constraint and I'm guessing artists are free
to use whatever they please. I especially loved the procedurally generated Rickroll [starts at around 12:00 mark](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ViHCotzWLDU).

Otherwise, work has been steady and I'm still recovering from illness. Hope to be back next week.
